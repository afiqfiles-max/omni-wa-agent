## 2. ITIL 4-Tier Escalation Framework
OmniTech Solutions implements a structured 4-tier service escalation architecture:
- **Tier 0 (Self-Service Automated RAG):** Immediate automated answers to standard inquiries, pricing, and FAQs.
- **Tier 1 (Guided Onboarding):** Interactive troubleshooting with documentation links and self-service references.
- **Tier 2 (Specialist Consultation & Direct Handoff):** Complex technical queries, contract adjustments, or explicit requests to speak with a human specialist. Handled via empathetic guidance and direct self-service contact links.
- **Tier 3 (Knowledge Gap Audit):** Unresolved questions are logged internally for review by our documentation curators to continuously improve knowledge base coverage.
- **Tier 4 (Critical Emergency Dispatch):** Severe production outages, critical security vulnerabilities, or active cyberfraud trigger an instant real-time dispatch ticket to the on-call engineering team via WhatsApp with a 1-hour idempotency safety guard.
