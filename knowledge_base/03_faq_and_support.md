# Frequently Asked Questions & Support Troubleshooting

## 1. Account & Subscription Management
- **Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my tier?**
  A: You can modify your subscription at any time directly through the Billing Settings in your Admin Portal. Changes take effect immediately with pro-rated billing.
- **Q: What happens if I exceed my monthly conversation quota?**
  A: Your service will never be interrupted. Overages are billed at a flat rate of $0.01 per additional conversation, or you can seamlessly upgrade to the next tier.

## 2. WhatsApp Integration & Anti-Ban Safeguards
- **Q: Can my WhatsApp business number get banned?**
  A: Our platform strictly enforces organic human pacing (Gaussian typing jitter of 15–25ms/char), read-to-type receipt delays, and per-user FIFO queues. For high-volume enterprise outbound marketing, we also support direct integration with the Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
- **Q: Can I connect multiple numbers?**
  A: Pro tiers support up to 3 numbers, while Enterprise tiers support unlimited multi-number routing per department.

## 3. Data Privacy & AI Grounding
- **Q: Does the AI hallucinate answers?**
  A: No. The AI agent operates under strict epistemic calibration. It only generates answers directly corroborated by your uploaded documents in the knowledge base. If an inquiry cannot be answered from your documents, it gracefully offers to connect the user with a human specialist.
- **Q: Is my company data used to train public models?**
  A: Absolutely not. All document embeddings and chat contexts are private, encrypted at rest, and never shared with third-party model training datasets.
